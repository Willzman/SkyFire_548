# Development realm news

What changed on the Development realm, newest first, in plain words for players.
The launcher shows the newest entries in its "What's new" panel.

Each change that reaches `Development` adds one line here, under the date it was
merged: what players will notice, not how it was done.

## 2026-11-26

- Dungeon Finder: a **Solo** checkbox next to Find Group (from the launcher's Solo Queue add-on). Tick it and you go into the dungeon by yourself, right away.
- Raid Finder groups are now proper 25-player raids (2 tanks, 6 healers, 17 damage) instead of 5-player groups.
- The party menu now shows the right dungeon and raid difficulty; no more "Dungeon Difficulty set to 10 Player" when a group forms.
- The Satchel of Helpful Goods from random dungeons always has an item for your class; it could open empty before.
- The dungeon reward window shows the right amount of money.
- Dungeon Finder groups of one: dying, teleporting out or hearthing no longer breaks up the group, so you can go back in and still get the reward.
- Dungeon Finder: the queue's role counts now match the smaller groups the realm allows.
- Dungeon Finder: the realm can start dungeons and scenarios with fewer than 5 (or 3) players.

## 2026-11-25

- Auction house bot: the realm can fill the auction houses with listings and buy players' auctions.
- Ulduar: all bosses use the newer scripts, including Thorim and Mimiron, with their hard modes, achievements and loot.
- Spell critical hits deal double damage, as they should.
- Spells and abilities use the right values for heroic and raid difficulties.
- A batch of 22 core fixes from SkyFire.

## 2026-11-24

- Arenas: rewards at the end of a season.
- Guild perk Working Overtime raises the chance of skill-ups.
- About 24,000 more creatures in the open world, where they were missing.
- Many more creatures now patrol, wander and fight with their own abilities, instead of standing still.
- Razorfen Downs: the bosses and the idol event work.
- Silverpine Forest: The Warchief Cometh scene plays for quest 26965.
- Nine old-world quests have their quest givers back.

## 2026-11-23

- Dungeons and raids: thousands of missing creatures and objects added, and the right ones per difficulty.
- New scripts for Icecrown Citadel (with the Gunship Battle), Ulduar, Naxxramas, Karazhan, The Nexus, Forge of Souls, Blackrock Depths, Black Temple, Slave Pens, Sunken Temple, Arcatraz and Zul'Aman.
- World bosses: the Sha of Anger moves between its spots, and Xuen is in his corner.
- Holiday events: more of their spells work (Love is in the Air, Lunar Festival and others).
